# Open your café

Open [the café capstone](https://agent.feikowielsma.nl/cafe) with your course code. No installation or personal service account is needed.

You have 30 minutes, €100, three menu slots and a lunch crowd to serve. Your café is private to your course account. All purchases and customers are fictional.

1. Spend five minutes choosing a name, concept and target customers. Inspect the recipes and supplier snapshot.
2. Give your agent a task. Choose one agent or three specialists with separate contexts and sequential handoffs. Try a different tool-call limit, or disconnect the supplier MCP to see what changes.
3. Inspect the storefront and ingredient proposal. Approve the spending yourself. Check quantities, prices and dietary choices.
4. Resolve the checkout checks, then approve opening. Place a test order from the storefront.
5. Introduce one surprise: oat milk becomes unavailable, the crowd becomes vegan, or supplier prices rise. Ask your agent to adapt. Approve any new purchases and reopen.
6. Run the lunch rush. Compare customers served, sales and unused ingredients. You can run up to three rushes; stock and cash carry forward. Download your café results before resetting.

The supplier catalogue is read through the course MCP service. Scenario updates override its base prices and availability. Purchases arrive immediately after human approval in this simulation. The storefront is a configurable working template; agents edit its name, tagline, theme and menu. `test_checkout` checks server rules, rather than automating a browser. Allergen labels come from fixed recipes and cannot be changed by the agent.

Each lunch rush has twelve customers with €4 or €6.50 budgets and a thirty-minute kitchen preparation allowance. Some customers require vegan choices or avoid milk. Remaining ingredient value is potential waste if you finish now. There is no single combined score: discuss the tradeoffs your café made.

For trainers: ask one team to prioritise accessibility and service, another to minimise waste, and another to create a distinctive concept. Sales, service performance, programme coordination and QA perspectives all have a role. Compare one-agent and specialist approaches. Ask which tool calls helped and which human decisions mattered. A surprise closes the café for review; menu or storefront edits also require approval to reopen.
