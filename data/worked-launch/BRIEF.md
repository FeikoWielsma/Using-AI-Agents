# Launch meeting brief

## Goal
Prepare an evidence-based decision brief for the 11 September launch review.
## Inputs
Use the current checklist, status updates, registers, site readiness and rollback plan.
## Deliverable
Produce supported facts, conflicting dates, unresolved conditions, owners and a draft update.
## Acceptance criteria
- Cite the record supporting each material claim.
- Distinguish a forecast from a commitment and an approval.
- Preserve missing evidence and ask specific follow-up questions.
## Authority
The responsible review owner decides. No communication is sent by this workspace.
