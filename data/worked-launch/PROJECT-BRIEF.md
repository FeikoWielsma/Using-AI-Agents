# Proposed workplace pilot

## Task and benefit
Prepare a weekly evidence-review brief so a human can find open conditions faster.
## Inputs and access
An approved copy of the current checklist, status records and decision log; read access only.
## Output
A sourced draft, open questions, compact memory and a reusable review procedure.
## Authority
A person reviews file changes and decides on business actions. Communication remains a reviewed draft.
## Pilot checks
Use completed, incomplete and contradictory test packs. Compare findings with an expert review.
## Measures
Preparation time, review time, missed conditions, unsupported claims and model usage.
## Open dependencies
Employer-approved tool, data access, responsible owner and operating costs need confirmation before a real pilot.
